# ShiftControl

Manage SaaS subscriptions from Claude. The included [refresh-subscription-info](skills/refresh-subscription-info/) skill reads invoices from connected email or Xero, matches them to apps in ShiftControl, and shows you proposed changes to costs, billing frequency, and contract terms. It sends updates to ShiftControl only after you approve them.

The plugin includes the ShiftControl MCP connector. Connect it with your ShiftControl account to give Claude access to the records you already have permission to use. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/ShiftControl-io/skills/blob/main/INSTALL.md) and [ShiftControl Privacy Policy](https://shiftcontrol.io/privacy-policy) for details.
