# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately by email to **security@shiftcontrol.io**. Do not open a public issue.

This covers:

- the skills and plugin files in this repository
- the ShiftControl MCP server at `mcp.shiftcontrol.io`, which the plugin connects to
- the ShiftControl service itself

Please include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and the impact you think it has. We will acknowledge your report, investigate it, and keep you updated until it is resolved.

Our security contact is also published at https://shiftcontrol.io/.well-known/security.txt.
